Profile

Analysis belongs
next to the build.

I’m pursuing placement in mechanical engineering at the University of Washington, with an intended applied mathematics minor.

I’m especially interested in mechanical design, fluid mechanics, and controls. Earlier work includes engineering graphics and CAD study at Edmonds College, projects with its Rocketry Club, and hands-on leadership as an Eagle Scout.

NOTE 01Build to expose constraints

Physical prototypes reveal mounting, access, wiring, and motion problems that are easy to miss on paper.

NOTE 02Measure without overselling

When a setup cannot support a reliable conclusion, that limitation belongs in the result.

NOTE 03Make decisions visible

A good case study explains why the design changed, not only what the final object looks like.
